Voice Casting Desk · Mere Voice

What this kit does

It takes you from "who could be the Voice?" to "the chosen voice has recorded everything we need", for the fixed lines and the generative layers. Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Steps

  1. Casting: write down 2–3 voices and give each a code (P1, P2, P3). Choose them with the dossier §51 source matrix in mind.
  2. Send each voice: 01 Audition recorder (they record in the browser, or open phone recordings, then press «دریافت بسته‌ی صدا» to get one ZIP) and the consent form. For people who only have a phone, send the PDF script; they record with the phone and send the files.
  3. Collect the ZIP packages or audio files they send. Use nothing before the signed consent is back.
  4. Listening room: open all packages together. Listen blind following the Protocol. Save your results.
  5. Full session: send the chosen voice 02 Full session recorder (or its PDF).
  6. Hand over: copy every received ZIP into mere-voice\recordings_inbox\, double-click IMPORT_RECORDINGS.bat, then tell Claude "the full session is in".

Before sending, you may want to change the two take directions: A «طبیعی» (natural), B «دور و بالینی» (distant, clinical). Ask Claude to regenerate the recorder pages with your wording.

Formats the recorders produce

  • Recorded in the page: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain are switched off, so the raw voice is kept.
  • Opened from a phone: the original file is kept byte for byte (m4a, wav, mp3…). Nothing is re-encoded.
  • The package also holds P#_ROOMTONE.wav (10 s of the room's own noise) and P#_manifest.json (every take's text, source, duration, peak level, clipping, and the performer's notes).
  • File names follow P1_VOICE-004_B.wav, which the project importer and this desk both read.

Pass criteria, fixed before listening

A · First hearingDid I understand every word, hearing it once? Any word I couldn't catch fails this gate. B · PronunciationDid any word sound wrong or odd (vowel, ezafe, colloquial form, drug name)? Write it down. C · ContinuityDoes VOICE-004 sound like one person forming one long thought? No restarts, no odd pauses, no flat recited list. D · BodyIs this usable as the Voice before any effects? What does it make the Voice be: narrator, hidden character, inner speech, institution, author…? (dossier §51)

A human take is not a synthetic engine

One slip in A or B is usually a retake (coach it, record again), not a reason to drop the person. Judge the voice and its presence mostly in D.

Procedure (20–30 minutes)

  1. Listening room: keep Blind and Match loudness on, and turn on Pass 1 (each sample plays once).
  2. Pass 1: play each sample once and note A and B.
  3. Turn Pass 1 off. Pass 2: listen freely and rate C and D, then give a verdict: KEEP / RETRY / DROP / HOLD.
  4. Press Save results. Only then turn Blind off to see who was who.
  5. If possible, repeat with a second native listener who doesn't know the candidates.

Decision rules

  • KEEP on at least 2 of 3 lines, and no unresolved C failure → strong candidate.
  • The same word wrong in both takes → coach and retake before judging.
  • Two voices pass → choose by D (your artistic call), or keep both, one per register or as "several apparent bodies" (dossier §50).
  • Nobody passes D → cast another voice. Don't process a weak voice into shape.

After the choice

  1. Send the chosen voice 02 Full session recorder. It covers all 17 lines × 2 takes, a varied reading for the voice model, free speech, and the Grave-Machine assembly pilot (about 1–1.5 hours; can be split across days).
  2. Put every ZIP they send into mere-voice\recordings_inbox\ and double-click IMPORT_RECORDINGS.bat.
  3. Tell Claude "the full session is in". Claude then:
    • adds a "recorded voice" source so the 17 lines play in the workbench;
    • assembles the 5 Grave-Machine test lines from recorded pieces;
    • tests voice conversion from the reading material;
    • sets up one blind round for the generative layer.

Privacy: the project repository on GitHub is public, and all recording folders are excluded from it.
