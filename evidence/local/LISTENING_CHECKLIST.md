# Listening & Windows-audio checklist (TASK-R02 / gates GATE-HUMAN-VOICE-LISTENING + GATE-WINDOWS-AUDIO-SMOKE)

Start the workbench: double-click `RUN_WORKBENCH.bat`, open http://127.0.0.1:8765/ (status should read `READY · PROCESSED`, 17 lines, 17 events).
Use your real headphones / default Windows output. About 15 minutes.

## 1. Persian pronunciation and prosody (Mana/Piper voice)
Mark each: OK / mispronounced word (write it) / rhythm wrong (write where).

- [ ] Opening, VOICE-001, VOICE-002 — «بدن زندگی رو پس می‌زنه…» / «نفس زندگی رو پس می‌زنه…»
- [ ] Clinical passage, VOICE-003, VOICE-004 — medication/anxiety/side-effects text (formal register, long words: watch «اضطراب», «سرگیجه»)
- [ ] VOICE-005 — «دارای تمامی علایم حیاتی ثابت…» (colloquial and clinical mixed)
- [ ] Rejection passage, VOICE-006 to VOICE-013 — the repeated «… را پس می‌زند» pattern; does the repetition sound intentional, not robotic?
- [ ] Late passage, VOICE-014 to VOICE-017 — short lines «لامسه دست را پس می‌زند.» … «لخته خون را پس می‌زند.»
